Sub2API Credit appearance: third-party notices

The Credit appearance adapts presentation from Credit (Apache-2.0) and CDK
(MIT). Their license texts are included in this directory. Sub2API's existing
project license continues to apply to Sub2API.

Bundled font families:
- Inter: FONTS-INTER-OFL.txt
- Noto Sans SC: FONTS-NOTO-OFL.txt
- Geist Mono: FONTS-GEIST-OFL.txt

The fonts are bundled locally as WOFF2 subsets and retain their original
licenses. These notices are shipped with the website, embedded application
and container image. Source and adaptation details: docs/custom/credit-style.md.
